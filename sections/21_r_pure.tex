\subsection{Pure ViT-B comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:vit_compare} isolates the comparison against prior
transformer-based methods using the same ViT-B/16 backbone family.

The 89.97\% mAP of our pipeline exceeds the CLIP-ReID (84.5\%) and TransReID*
(82.3\%) entries above, but---as stressed throughout---those are raw-embedding
numbers whereas ours includes AQE $+$ $k$-reciprocal re-ranking $+$ TTA. At
matched no-post-processing conditions our embedding (82.2\%) is on par with
TransReID and below CLIP-ReID, so this is a post-processing gap.
Section~\ref{sec:matched} runs the matched-condition comparison directly and
finds that TransReID, given our identical post-processing, in fact
\emph{exceeds} our pipeline (91.12\% vs.\ 89.97\% mAP). For clarity,
Table~\ref{tab:oppoints} summarises every reported operating point in one
place, including a \emph{validation-selected} fusion row
(Section~\ref{sec:holdout}) as the recommended tuning-free default. The
headline fusion also attains \textbf{84.14\% mINP}~\cite{ye2022survey},
recomputed deterministically from the released per-query dumps.
