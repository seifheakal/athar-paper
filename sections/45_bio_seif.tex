\noindent\textbf{Seif T. Heakal} received his B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering, Cairo University, Giza, Egypt. He led this project from start to finish and carried out most of the work. This includes the model training, the post-processing pipeline, the matched-condition and cross-dataset studies, the cost and significance analyses, and the writing of the manuscript. His work focuses on evaluation methodology for re-identification systems, on the reproducibility of empirical results, and on optimization, including efficient re-ranking and post-processing. His research interests include computer vision, optimization, benchmark methodology and trustworthy machine-learning evaluation.
