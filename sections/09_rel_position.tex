\subsection{Positioning}
To the best of our knowledge, no prior work reports above 90\% mAP on VeRi-776 with a pure ViT-B backbone. The best prior pure-ViT result is 84.5\% mAP~\cite{li2023clipreid,opencodepapers2024veri}, reported without post-processing. Our post-processing changes this. We apply it to the official TransReID and CLIP-ReID checkpoints. They reach \textbf{91.12\%} and \textbf{91.80\% mAP}. These are the highest pure ViT-B results we know of on VeRi-776, and they are above our own 89.97\% (Section~\ref{sec:matched}). Our recipe does not beat these two baselines by itself. Without post-processing, our embedding (82.2\% mAP) does not beat CLIP-ReID. But our post-processing pushes both baselines further than any published pure ViT-B result. This is the main point of our paper. Post-processing adds 7.75\,pp mAP to our ViT-B stream (82.22\% to 89.97\%) with no retraining. AQE costs almost nothing. Re-ranking costs more, but the sparse implementation keeps its memory small (Section~\ref{sec:cost}). For the fusion result, no prior two-stream method exceeds 93.3\% mAP on VeRi-776. The best prior single-stream result is 92.9\% mAP~\cite{lu2025clipsenet}. Our fusion beats it by a narrow $+0.42$\,pp.
