\medskip\noindent\textbf{Mohamed A. Ali} received his B.Sc. in Computer
Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering, Cairo University, Giza, Egypt. His
undergraduate research addressed data augmentation and optimizer selection for
vision transformers under limited-identity training regimes. He contributed to
the augmentation pipeline, the seed-variance study and the loss-function
comparison reported in this paper. His research interests include deep
learning, optimization, and intelligent transportation systems.
