\subsection{Qualitative analysis}
Visual inspection suggests that the fusion model handles frontal-to-rear viewpoint changes, illumination shifts and partial occlusion well. Failures happen in three cases. (a)~Near-identical vehicles of the same make, model and colour at similar viewpoints, where both streams return similar-looking but incorrect identities. (b)~Extreme occlusion ($>$60\% of the vehicle body). (c)~Very low-resolution gallery crops. In these cases, both streams tend to fail on the same queries. This points to correlated errors, not complementary ones, in the hardest cases. Table~\ref{tab:qual} summarises these failure modes together with representative success cases. Full retrieval panel figures are in the project repository.
