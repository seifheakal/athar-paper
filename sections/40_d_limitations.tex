\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The full post-processing pipeline (AQE $+$ re-ranking) has $\mathcal{O}(N^{2})$ complexity in gallery size $N$ in its dense form. It is mainly suited to offline evaluation, not real-time deployment. The sparse implementation of Section~\ref{sec:cost} removes the memory wall, but not the quadratic growth in time.
  \item The fusion strategy needs two inference passes. This roughly doubles memory and latency compared with a single-stream model.
  \item Cross-dataset transfer is reported in Section~\ref{sec:veriwild}. Applied zero-shot to all three VeRi-Wild protocols, the ViT-B embedding transfers in part (35.9\,/\,30.5\,/\,23.8\% mAP with AQE at 3{,}000\,/\,5{,}000\,/\,10{,}000 identities, competitive with published VehicleID-sourced transfer). But the two-stream fusion does not generalise. At the VeRi-776-tuned weight it falls below the stronger single stream at every scale, and $k$-reciprocal re-ranking also reduces transfer accuracy. This agrees with the CityFlowV2 evidence, where a direct port of the same fusion produced a steady drop in MTMC IDF1, with a best-case change of about $-1.8$\,pp at higher fusion weights. Together these suggest that the small VeRi-776 advantage of the fusion is domain-specific. Section~\ref{sec:veriwild} shows a first mitigation. Training-free query-adaptive weighting recovers single-stream transfer accuracy (35.9 vs.\ 32.8\% mAP with a fixed weight). A ground-truth per-query oracle (40.1\%) shows the headroom for learned query-adaptive fusion, which we leave for future work. Absolute VeRi-Wild accuracy is also low in this no-adaptation setting, and we report no fine-tuned cross-dataset numbers.
  \item Our evaluation follows the standard random-identity splits of VeRi-776 and VeRi-Wild. Recent analysis shows that these splits contain many visually near-duplicate identities across train and test. It also shows that mAP on vehicle types held out of training drops sharply (from above 90\% to 65--70\%), even for state-of-the-art models~\cite{benmabrouk2026genlimits}. We did not evaluate under such a seen/unseen-type protocol. Our in-domain numbers should be read as standard-protocol results. The zero-shot VeRi-Wild transfer of Section~\ref{sec:veriwild} is our only probe of generalisation beyond the training distribution.
  \item The exact $P \times K$ identity-sampling tuple used to train the released Stream~1 checkpoint was not logged (Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}). So we cannot specify from-scratch retraining exactly from our records. Retraining reproducibility is bounded by the $\pm0.55$\,pp seed band of Section~\ref{sec:seed_variance}. We also did not run a controlled comparison of 256\,$\times$\,256 training resolution against the deployed 224\,$\times$\,224. It would require retraining.
\end{enumerate}
