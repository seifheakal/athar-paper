\subsection{Ablation study}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} traces the complete performance trajectory,
visualised in Figures~\ref{fig:trajectory} and~\ref{fig:isolated}. Endpoint
values are verified against published results~\cite{he2021transreid} and
repository experiment logs; all intermediate rows are measured from isolated
ablation experiments.

The isolated-component block clarifies which ingredients our recipe depends on,
relative to the un-tuned A1 control. AdamW with layer-wise LR decay (A4) gives
the largest movement, $+23.29$\,pp over the un-tuned SGD control A1; CLIP
initialisation (A2) is next at $+13.35$\,pp. We stress that the A4 delta
reflects the difficulty of tuning SGD under our short schedule rather than an
intrinsic $+23$\,pp superiority of AdamW over SGD---a fully tuned SGD recipe
(TransReID) already reaches 82.3\% mAP~\cite{he2021transreid}. What A4
establishes is that AdamW with layer-wise LR decay reaches a strong operating
point without bespoke per-optimiser schedule tuning, which is the
practical property the recipe relies on. SupCon in isolation (A3) barely moves
the needle ($+1.86$\,pp) but combines well once CLIP and AdamW are in place:
the deployed A5$\alpha$ recipe reaches 89.06\% mAP, statistically
indistinguishable from the alternative A5$\beta$ recipe (88.57\% mAP)---a
0.49\,pp single-run difference that lies within the $\pm 0.55$\,pp three-seed
standard deviation of A5$\alpha$ (88.75\,$\pm$\,0.55\,pp;
Section~\ref{sec:seed_variance}). Within the cumulative trajectory, the most
impactful post-processing step is $k$-reciprocal
re-ranking~\cite{zhong2017rerank} on Stream~1 ($+5.55$\,pp);
AQE~\cite{arandjelovic2012aqe} adds $+1.86$\,pp; TTA contributes $+0.34$\,pp.
Fusion provides $+1.88$\,pp above the stronger post-processed CLIP-SENet
stream, confirming genuine complementarity between the two feature spaces.
