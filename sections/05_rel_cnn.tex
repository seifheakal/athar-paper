\subsection{CNN-era vehicle re-identification}
Liu et al.~\cite{liu2016veri} introduced the VeRi-776 benchmark. PROVID~\cite{liu2018provid} extended it. PROVID uses appearance, licence plates, camera layout and time cues. It gets 53.42\% mAP and 81.56\% Rank-1.\footnote{The PROVID
(53.42/81.56) and VAMI (50.13/77.03) figures are the values reported in the
respective papers and reproduced throughout the survey
literature~\cite{ye2022survey,li2024vehiclereidsurvey}.} Ye et al.~\cite{ye2022survey} wrote a survey of deep person Re-ID. Yi et al.~\cite{li2024vehiclereidsurvey} survey work on vehicles. They cover parts, multiple data sources and cross-domain adaptation.

Table~\ref{tab:cnn_era} lists the main CNN-era methods on VeRi-776, with the idea behind each one and the result reported in its paper.

\begin{table}[!ht]
  \centering
  \caption{CNN-era methods on VeRi-776, as reported in the original papers}
  \label{tab:cnn_era}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{L{3.3cm}L{2.5cm}L{4.9cm}C{1.2cm}C{1.2cm}}
    \toprule
    Method & Venue & Main idea & mAP~(\%) & R1~(\%) \\
    \midrule
    VAMI~\cite{zhou2018vami} & CVPR 2018 & Generates other viewpoints with adversarial training & 50.13 & 77.03 \\
    PVEN~\cite{meng2020pven} & CVPR 2020 & View-aware features for each part, from semantic segmentation & 79.5 & 95.6 \\
    CAL~\cite{rao2021cal} & ICCV 2021 & Attention that ignores background cues & 74.3 & 95.4 \\
    VehicleNet~\cite{zheng2020vehiclenet} & IEEE TMM 2021 & Trains on VeRi-776, VehicleID and synthetic data together & 83.41 & 96.78 \\
    HPGN~\cite{shen2022hpgn} & IEEE T-ITS 2022 & Pyramid graph network for spatial information at different scales & 80.18 & 96.72 \\
    A Strong Baseline~\cite{cybercore2021strong} & CVPR-W AICity 2021 & ResNet101-IBN-a with multi-head attention, trained on several domains & 87.1 & -- \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

A Strong Baseline is a strong result for ResNet models before transformers. Khorramshahi et al.~\cite{khorramshahi2023selfsupervision} test self-supervised pretraining on ResNet, ViT, Swin and ConvNeXt backbones in the AI City setting.
