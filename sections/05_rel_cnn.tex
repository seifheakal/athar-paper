\subsection{CNN-era vehicle re-identification}
Liu et al.~\cite{liu2016veri} introduced the VeRi-776 benchmark. PROVID~\cite{liu2018provid} extended it. PROVID uses appearance, licence plates, camera layout and time cues. It gets 53.42\% mAP and 81.56\% Rank-1.\footnote{The PROVID
(53.42/81.56) and VAMI (50.13/77.03) figures are the values reported in the
respective papers and reproduced throughout the survey
literature~\cite{ye2022survey,li2024vehiclereidsurvey}.} Ye et al.~\cite{ye2022survey} wrote a survey of deep Re-ID for people and vehicles. Li et al.~\cite{li2024vehiclereidsurvey} survey work on vehicles. They cover parts, multiple data sources and cross-domain adaptation.

VAMI~\cite{zhou2018vami} generates other viewpoints of a vehicle with adversarial training. It was published at CVPR 2018 and gets 50.13\% mAP and 77.03\% Rank-1.
PVEN~\cite{meng2020pven} uses semantic segmentation to get view-aware features for each part. It was published at CVPR 2020 and gets 79.5\% mAP and 95.6\% Rank-1.
CAL~\cite{rao2021cal} uses attention to ignore background cues. It was published at ICCV 2021 and gets 74.3\% mAP and 95.4\% Rank-1.
VehicleNet~\cite{zheng2020vehiclenet} trains on several datasets at once, VeRi-776, VehicleID and synthetic data. It was published in IEEE TMM 2020 and gets 83.41\% mAP and 96.78\% Rank-1.
HPGN~\cite{shen2022hpgn} uses a pyramid graph network to model spatial information at different image scales. It was published in IEEE T-ITS 2022 and gets 80.18\% mAP and 96.72\% Rank-1.
A Strong Baseline~\cite{cybercore2021strong} uses ResNet101-IBN-a with multi-head attention and trains on several domains. It was published at CVPR-W AICity 2021 and gets 87.1\% mAP. This is a strong result for ResNet models before transformers.
Khorramshahi et al.~\cite{khorramshahi2023selfsupervision} test self-supervised pretraining on ResNet, ViT, Swin and ConvNeXt backbones in the AI City setting.
